I'm Jerome (Zhenyu) Liu, a Technical Success Manager at [TomTom](https://www.tomtom.com/) in Shanghai, working in CX Delivery Management with automotive and platform customers across Asia-Pacific on TomTom's navigation SDKs, map content and location APIs.

Before TomTom I spent several years in cloud computing. At Microsoft I was an Azure Technical Support Engineer and later a Technical Advisor, focusing on Azure Storage, Azure Cache for Redis, Service Fabric and the Azure SDKs, and acting as the team's technical lead. Most recently I worked in warehouse-automation robotics at HAI Robotics as an L2 Support Expert, handling escalations from field teams and key customers and leading on-site support for major events such as SHEIN's Black Friday peak.

I hold a Master of Data Science from Monash University and a BEng in Network Engineering from Inner Mongolia University.

What I do
======
* **Escalation and incident handling** for enterprise customers: cloud outages, robotics production lines, navigation and map-data defects.
* **Technical enablement**: customer-facing training, internal troubleshooting guides and tooling used by global support teams.
* **Support tooling and AI workflows**: diagnostic toolkits, escalation automation and LLM-based case analysis that cut manual effort in support operations.

Outside work I build small tools for myself, mostly around maps, macOS and home automation. A few are on the [Portfolio](/portfolio/) page.
